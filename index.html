Research question

How can vector-quantized representations support the estimation of camera intrinsics from visual observations?

Research focus

I investigated vector-quantized representations for monocular calibration, connecting representation design with camera geometry.

Research context +

CameraVQ connects my interest in representation learning to a geometric inference task. The work motivates broader questions about how representations can support 3D perception beyond a single application domain. First-author research · M.S. thesis · Advisor: Jung Hyun Han.
